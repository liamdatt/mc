# 3. Company History & Positioning

## Origin Story
Minott Equipment & Chemicals Limited is the brainchild of founder Chester G. Minott. The company opened its doors over 35 years ago, focusing primarily on the distribution of cleaning equipment.

## Evolution
From its origins as a cleaning equipment distributor, the company expanded into chemical manufacturing. Today it is positioned as an industry leader in:
- Manufacturing of household chemicals
- Manufacturing of industrial chemicals
- Distribution of cleaning equipment
- Distribution of sanitation supplies
- Distribution of janitorial supplies

## Self-Positioning Claims
- Markets itself as "Jamaica's Largest Supplier of Chemicals" (page title meta)
- "The number one source for commercial chemicals, cleaning equipment, sanitation & janitorial supplies" (Facebook page tagline)
- Industry leader in chemical manufacturing and equipment distribution
- Over 35 years of providing chemical manufacturing and equipment distribution throughout Jamaica

## Core Value Proposition
Commitment to quality and exceptional service: "As a manufacturer of our own extensive line of chemical products we pride ourselves on our dedication to quality and our commitment to exceptional service. It is this attitude of excellence that is the lifeblood of Minott Equipment & Chemicals Limited."

## Third-Party Claims (not on current site, but referenced in directories)
- Chemicals are manufactured on-site at the company's plant
- Reported as ISO certified (per Fiwibusiness directory listing)
- Earth-friendly chemicals safe for daycare facilities and Homes for the Aged
- Strong enough for restaurants and meets government specifications for destroying bacteria

# 4. Brand Partnerships – Elite Distributor Status

The company sources from over fifteen (15) overseas suppliers in the United States and China, plus a host of local Jamaican suppliers. This diversified sourcing strategy is highlighted as enabling dependable supply and competitive pricing.
