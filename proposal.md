# Website Proposal — Minott Equipment & Chemicals Limited

**Prepared for:** Minott Equipment & Chemicals Limited
**Date:** June 5, 2026

---

## Overview

This proposal summarizes the website we have built for Minott Equipment & Chemicals Limited and outlines the next phase of enhancements. The site is a modern, animation-rich marketing and product platform designed to showcase your chemical and janitorial supply business, capture customer inquiries, and give your team full control over the product catalog — all from a single, easy-to-manage system.

---

## What's Included Today

### 1. Marketing Website

A polished, multi-page site that presents the brand with premium motion design throughout:

- **Home** — animated hero, company highlights, and product category showcase
- **About** — company story and values
- **Solutions** — industry-focused overview of what you offer and who you serve
- **Contact** — contact form that delivers messages straight to your admin inbox

The site is fully responsive (desktop, tablet, mobile), uses your brand palette and typography, and includes smooth-scroll and scroll-triggered animations — with full accessibility support (animations automatically reduce for users who prefer less motion).

### 2. Product Listing Database

The entire product catalog is driven by a real database — not hard-coded pages — so your inventory stays current without developer involvement:

- **Category browsing** (`/products`) with a category landing page for each product line
- **Individual product pages** with descriptions, specifications, and calls to action
- **Browse-all view** across the full catalog
- The site navigation's product dropdown updates automatically as categories are added or changed

### 3. Quote Builder

Customers can build a quote request as they browse:

- "Add to Quote" buttons on product pages
- A persistent quote cart that follows the customer across the site
- A quote review page (`/quote`) where customers submit their request with contact details
- **Sample requests** available directly from product pages

### 4. Admin Panel

A password-protected back office (`/admin`) where your team manages the site:

- **Products** — create, edit, and remove products
- **Categories** — manage the product category structure
- **Requests inbox** — every quote request, sample request, and contact message lands in one unified inbox for follow-up

Access is secured with encrypted session authentication.

### 5. AI Chatbot Widget

An AI-powered chat assistant (OneChat) is integrated site-wide:

- Answers visitor questions about your products and business in real time
- Connected to the live product database through a dedicated set of read-only API endpoints (categories, product search, and product detail), so its answers reflect your actual, current catalog
- Loads lazily so it never slows down the page experience

### 6. Public Product API

A set of public, read-only API endpoints exposes the catalog data (categories, product search, product details). Today this powers the AI chatbot; it also positions the site for future integrations (e.g., other tools that need live product data).

## Planned Enhancements (Next Phase)

### 1. Google Analytics

Full visitor analytics integration so you can see:

- How many people visit the site and where they come from
- Which products and pages get the most attention
- How visitors move through the site before requesting a quote

This gives you the data to make informed marketing and inventory decisions.

### 2. Client Portal — Quote History

A secure, login-based portal for your customers:

- Customers create an account and sign in
- They can view their **previous quote requests** and their status in one place
- Lays the groundwork for future self-service features (reordering, saved product lists)
